Healthy Foundation

Specialist Gum Care & Periodontal Therapy in Bangalore

Healthy gums are the foundation of a lasting smile. Bleeding gums, bad breath (halitosis), and swollen or receding gums are key signs of underlying periodontal disease. At OraFix Dental Care, we provide comprehensive, non-surgical and surgical gum therapies to halt bacterial progression and preserve bone support.

Patients looking for gentle, effective periodontics in HBR Layout trust Dr. Sheema M. for ethical diagnosis, ultrasonic scaling, and long-term periodontal wellness.

Treatments

Gum Treatment Options

From routine maintenance to intensive deep root cleaning, our personalized treatments restore gum health.

Deep Cleaning (Scaling & Root Planing)

Non-surgical therapy that meticulously removes plaque, hardened tartar (calculus), and bacteria below the gum line, smoothing root surfaces to allow natural gum reattachment.

Gum Disease & Gingivitis Treatment

Customized antimicrobial rinses, localized medication, and ultrasonic therapy to eliminate active gum infections and reverse early-stage gingivitis.

Periodontal Maintenance

Scheduled 3-to-6 month supportive maintenance visits with specialized pocket monitoring to ensure periodontal disease stays completely under control.

Why Gum Care Matters

Benefits of Professional Periodontal Therapy

Catching gum disease early preserves your natural teeth and protects your systemic health.

Stops gum inflammation, sensitivity, and bleeding
Prevents tooth loosening and alveolar bone destruction
Eliminates chronic bad breath caused by subgingival bacteria
Helps maintain long-term oral stability and natural smile confidence
