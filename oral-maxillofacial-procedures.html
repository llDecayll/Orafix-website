Surgical Precision

Expert Oral Surgery & Wisdom Teeth Removal in Bangalore

When teeth cannot be saved or become dangerously impacted, surgical dental management is vital to relieve acute pain, protect adjoining teeth, and avoid severe infection. At OraFix Dental Care, we specialize in minimally invasive, painless oral surgery techniques.

Our experienced dental surgeon conducts 3D radiographic evaluations, ensuring safe nerve clearance, smooth extractions, and rapid recovery.

Procedures

Oral Surgery Treatment Options

We explain every procedure, recovery expectation, and cost clearly before commencing treatment.

Wisdom Teeth Removal

Recommended when wisdom teeth are impacted, infected, or crowding adjacent teeth. Proper clinical evaluation ensures safe, painless removal and rapid recovery.

Surgical Tooth Extractions

When a severely broken or deeply decayed tooth cannot be restored, we perform gentle extractions under effective local anesthesia to protect bone structure.

Impacted Teeth Management

Relieves chronic pressure, pericoronitis pain, and recurrent infection caused by teeth that fail to erupt fully into proper position.

Oral Biopsy Procedures

Specialized minor tissue biopsy performed to evaluate abnormal oral lesions, patches, or cysts for definitive diagnostic peace of mind.

Clinical Benefits

Benefits of Oral & Maxillofacial Procedures

Timely surgical intervention removes chronic sources of pain and restores oral wellness.

Relieves acute pain caused by infection or impacted teeth
Prevents the spread of infection and damage to surrounding teeth
Safely manages complex root configurations and impactions
Supports smoother healing with comprehensive post-operative care
