Treatment Options

Orthodontic Treatments We Offer

Modern alignment options tailored to your lifestyle, comfort, and aesthetic preferences.

Braces Treatment

Metal and ceramic braces help correct teeth alignment and bite irregularities, improving chewing efficiency, speech clarity, and appearance.

Invisalign & Clear Aligners

Invisalign and clear aligner systems provide a nearly invisible, removable alternative to traditional braces—perfect for discreet smile correction.

Custom Retainers

After active alignment, custom retainers maintain your new smile and prevent teeth from shifting back, guaranteeing long-lasting stability.

Bite Correction & Jaw Alignment

Corrects overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites to reduce jaw joint (TMJ) strain and optimize chewing mechanics.
