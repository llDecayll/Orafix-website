What You Need to Know About Wisdom Teeth & Safe Extraction

Home / Blog / Wisdom Teeth
By Dr. Sheema M. January 13, 2026 5 min read

Wisdom teeth, also known as third molars, are the last set of adult teeth to erupt, typically appearing between the ages of 17 and 25. For some fortunate individuals, they emerge smoothly and align properly with other teeth. However, for a majority of people, modern jaws lack sufficient space, causing these teeth to become impacted, crooked, or trapped under the gum tissue.

Common Signs of an Impacted Wisdom Tooth

If a wisdom tooth is unable to emerge naturally, it can trigger a range of uncomfortable symptoms, including:

    Throbbing pain or tenderness at the very back of your jaw
    Red, swollen, or bleeding gums (Pericoronitis)
    Difficulty opening your mouth fully or pain while chewing
    Chronic bad breath and foul taste in the mouth

Why Is Wisdom Teeth Extraction Often Recommended?

Leaving an impacted wisdom tooth untreated can damage adjacent healthy second molars. Food debris and plaque easily become lodged in partially erupted flaps of gum, causing rapid cavities and deep bone loss. In rare instances, fluid-filled cysts can form around impacted crowns, weakening the jaw bone.

Is Wisdom Tooth Removal Painful?

At OraFix Dental Care, patient comfort is our highest priority. The procedure is performed under modern, highly effective local anesthesia, ensuring you feel zero sharp pain during extraction. Most standard extractions take less than 30 to 45 minutes.

Post-Operative Care & Recovery Tips

Smooth healing is easily achieved by following a few simple guidelines:

    Apply a cold ice pack to the cheek for the first 24 hours to reduce swelling
    Stick to soft, cool foods like yogurt, soups, and smoothies
    Avoid using drinking straws or smoking to prevent dry socket (loss of blood clot)
    Gently rinse with warm salt water starting 24 hours after surgery
