Urgent Dental Care

Fast, Gentle Relief for Acute Dental Emergencies in Bangalore

Persistent tooth pain, dental trauma, and sudden swelling require immediate attention to identify the underlying cause and prevent serious complications. At OraFix Dental Care, we provide same-day urgent care to stabilize damage, eliminate discomfort, and protect your teeth.

Whether you are dealing with a knocked-out tooth, lost filling, cracked crown, or painful abscess, our team is equipped to provide immediate, safe interventions.

Urgent Treatments

Emergency Dental Treatments We Provide

Timely treatment protects damaged teeth and supports rapid recovery with minimal complications.

Toothache Relief

Sudden, pulsating toothache relief to alleviate intense pain, diagnose infection, and treat root nerve inflammation quickly.

Broken or Knocked-Out Teeth

Prompt management for fractured, chipped, or avulsed teeth. Rapid re-implantation and bonding help save your natural tooth.

Dental Infections & Abscesses

Urgent abscess drainage, antibiotics prescription, and anti-inflammatory therapy to control swelling and stop the spread of infection.

Lost Fillings or Crowns

Emergency recementation or replacement for loose or lost restorations to prevent acute temperature sensitivity and further decay.

Why Act Quickly

Benefits of Timely Emergency Dental Care

Seeking immediate professional dental care prevents minor dental emergencies from escalating into severe, irreversible problems.

Immediate relief from severe pain and swelling
Prevention of infection spreading to surrounding teeth or jaw
High probability of saving traumatized or knocked-out teeth
Faster recovery and reduced need for complex surgery
