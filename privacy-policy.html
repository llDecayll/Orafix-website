Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 29-01-2026

At OraFix Dental Care, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal and dental information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or use our dental services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal details such as your name, phone number, email address, dental concerns, appointment preferences, and medical history when you submit an online booking form or contact us.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

    Schedule and confirm your dental appointments
    Respond to your treatment enquiries and medical queries
    Maintain accurate dental clinical records
    Improve our clinic services and patient experience

3. Sharing of Information

OraFix Dental Care does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information is only shared when necessary for clinical referrals with specialist doctors or as required by law.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate administrative and technical security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
